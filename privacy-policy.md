# ColorSight Privacy Policy

**Last updated: May 2026**

ColorSight does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. 
All color identification is performed entirely on your device using a bundled 
color database. No camera data, images, or color history ever leaves your device. 
The app requires no account, has no analytics, and makes no network requests. 
Your data is yours — it stays on your phone.

**Contact:** colorsightapp@gmail.com
